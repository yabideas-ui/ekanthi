Ekanthi — AI & Child Safety

How Ekanthi uses AI and keeps students safe · Last updated: 27 November 2026

Ekanthi Agent is an AI

Ekanthi includes Ekanthi Agent, an AI study helper that answers study doubts in Malayalam, Hindi and English. It is not a person. The app says this on the Agent screen. Like any AI, it can make mistakes — students should check important answers with their textbook or their teacher.

Which AI we use

  • For school students, Ekanthi Agent uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, under Anthropic's rules for products used by people under 18.
  • Tutor and administrator tools, and courses for adults (such as Kerala PSC and Railway), may use other AI services.
  • Students' questions are sent to the AI provider only to create the answer. We do not sell students' data or use it for advertising.

How we keep students safe

  • Studies only. The Agent is told to stay on studies, exams and study habits, and to refuse anything unsafe or not suitable for children.
  • No personal details. The Agent never asks for a student's address, phone number, photos, passwords or social media, and never suggests meeting anyone.
  • Help when it matters. If a student writes about self-harm, abuse, bullying or danger, the Agent replies kindly, tells them to speak to a parent or teacher right away, and shares the free Tele-MANAS helpline 14416 (24×7).
  • Safety flags. Worrying messages are flagged so the tutor can check on the student.
  • Daily limits. Each student has a daily limit on AI questions, set by the administrator.
  • Parents can see. Parents get their own login to see their child's activity, study time and progress.
  • Accounts are created or approved by us. Students log in with a username and PIN given by Ekanthi or approved by the administrator.

For parents

You can see your child's study time, tests and activity in the parent app. If anything in an AI answer worries you, please tell us and we will look at it the same day.
