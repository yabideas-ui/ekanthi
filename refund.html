For plans and extras bought on www.ekanthi.in · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Summary

  • Paid by mistake twice, or money taken but the plan was not switched on? Full refund.
  • Changed your mind? Ask within 3 days of payment and before using the paid features much (no more than 2 paid tests) — full refund.
  • After that, fees for the running period are not refundable, but you can ask to move the remaining days to a sibling or pause once for up to 15 days (for example, illness).

Extras

  • Support sessions with the tutor: unused sessions can be refunded within 30 days of payment.
  • Extra tests: unused tests can be refunded within 7 days.
  • Digital downloads: not refundable once the download link has been opened, unless the file is wrong or does not open — then we replace it or refund.

Cancellation

Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel — the plan simply ends at the end of the period you paid for. You can ask us to close your account at any time (see Delete account).
