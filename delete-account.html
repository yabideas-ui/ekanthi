Ekanthi — Delete your account or data

How to ask

A student account, a parent account, or any of its data can be deleted at any time. A parent can ask for a child's account.

  1. Send an email to helloekanthi@gmail.com (or message Ma'am on WhatsApp).
  2. Write the student's name and username (or the parent's username), and whether you want the whole account deleted or only some data (for example uploaded answer photos or doubts).
  3. We confirm it is really you (usually by replying from the parent's registered WhatsApp number) and delete it within 7 days. We will message you when it is done.

What is deleted

The account (name, username, PIN/password), class and subject choices, check-ins, quiz/test/homework answers and marks, uploaded photos, doubts and messages, app-usage records, notification tokens and the linked parent login.

It is removed from the live database right away, and from backups within 30 days. Nothing is kept except what the law requires (for example fee receipts, if any).
