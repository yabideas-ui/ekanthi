Ekanthi — Privacy Policy

Ekanthi (www.ekanthi.in) — a brand of DHANU GLOBAL ENTERPRISES, Kerala, India · for students, exam candidates, parents and tutors · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

Ekanthi is a private tuition and study-coaching app run by JP (Cyber Kalari). It is used by students who are enrolled with us, their parents and our tutors. Accounts are created by the tutor; there is no public sign-up.

Contact for any privacy question or request: support@ekanthi.in

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Student name, username, class, subjects; parent name, WhatsApp number, country and school (given by the parent to the tutor, or typed in the registration form with the parent's agreement)To run the student's account, show the right lessons and let the tutor and parent contact each other.
Login PIN / passwordTo sign in. Stored only as a secure one-way hash; nobody can read it.
Study activity: check-ins, quiz and test answers and scores, homework, revision requests, time spent in the app, when the app was openedTo show progress, give marks, make study plans and reminders, and show progress to the tutor and the student's parent.
Photos of written answers that the student chooses to uploadSo the tutor (and the app's AI checker) can mark the answer.
Questions and doubts typed to "Ekanthi Agent" (AI helper) or sent to the tutorTo answer the doubt. The tutor can see doubts to help the student.
Device type, operating system, browser / app versionTo support the app and show the tutor which students use the app.
Approximate city (from the internet connection, not GPS)To show the local weather and the right holidays. We do not use GPS or precise location.
Payment requests: chosen plan or extra, amount, and the transaction / reference number the user types (website only)So the tutor can check the payment in her bank / UPI app and switch the plan on. We never see or store card, bank or UPI PIN details.
Notification token (only if notifications are turned on)To send study reminders and alerts.
Exam-safety signals during tests (leaving the app, screenshot attempts on Android, very fast answers)To keep tests fair. These are shown to the tutor.

We do not collect contacts, call logs, SMS, precise location, advertising IDs or payment card details. The app has no ads and no in-app purchases. Plans are paid on the website (Razorpay or UPI / bank), never inside the Android app; new registrations are checked by the tutor before the account opens.

Who can see the information

  • The student sees their own work. Students never see other students' details (except first names on the class "toppers" card).
  • The student's parent (if the tutor gives them a parent login) sees that child's progress, marks, homework and app usage.
  • The tutor / admin sees all their students' study information to teach and support them.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Cloudflare (servers and database), GitHub (website hosting), Razorpay (online payments — they receive the payment details you type on their payment page; we only get the payment ID and status), and Google Gemini or Cloudflare Workers AI (to create notes and questions and to answer doubts; the text of the doubt or answer is sent to them for processing). They may not use it for their own purposes.

We never sell or rent personal information and never use it for advertising.

Children

Most of our users are school children. Accounts are created by the tutor only after the parent agrees to the child joining our tuition. We collect only what is needed for studying, we show no ads, and parents can see their child's progress. A parent can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete their child's information.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep study records while the student is with us. When an account is deleted, the student's personal details and study records are removed from the live database within 30 days (backups are overwritten within 30 more days). Login security records are kept for up to 30 days.

To delete an account or data, see Delete your account or data.

Security

All connections use encrypted HTTPS. PINs and passwords are stored only as hashes. Accounts lock after repeated wrong PINs. On the Android app, screenshots of student screens are blocked to protect test papers.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell users in the app.
